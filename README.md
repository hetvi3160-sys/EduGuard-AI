# EduGuard-AI
AI-powered Academic Dropout Prediction and Early Intervention System
​Project Objective
​EduGuard AI is an AI-powered Academic Dropout Prediction and Early Intervention System. Our vision is to shift the educational approach from reactive to proactive. By answering the question, "What if we could identify a student at risk before they actually drop out?", this system empowers educators to predict, understand, and intervene early, ultimately helping students stay on track and succeed.
​Key Features
​Proactive Risk Prediction: Analyzes critical data points like attendance, academic scores, assignment completion, participation, socio-economic status, and backlogs to accurately estimate dropout risk.
​Explainable AI: Ensures complete transparency by highlighting the specific factors driving a student's risk level, building educator trust in the AI's insights.
​Actionable Interventions: Goes beyond prediction by recommending targeted support strategies such as mentoring, counseling, or academic follow-ups.
​Centralized Dashboard: Gives educators a quick, comprehensive overview of analyzed students, high-risk cases, and proposed interventions in one place.
​Dataset Upload: Supports batch processing, allowing institutions to monitor entire classrooms or batches simultaneously rather than manually entering data.

​Backend: Python (FastAPI)
