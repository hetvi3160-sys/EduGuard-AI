ℹ️ About EduGuard AI

🎓 EduGuard AI

EduGuard is an early-warning system designed to identify students who may be at risk of academic dropout.

🔗 Core Pipeline

1
Student Data
Attendance, Academic Score, Assignment Rate, Participation, Socio-Economic Status, Backlogs
2
Feature Normalization
Features are converted into a comparable 0–1 scale.
3
Logistic Regression
A logistic regression model (with L2 regularization) predicts the probability of dropout.
4
Risk Classification
LOW (< 30%) • MEDIUM (30%–59%) • HIGH (≥ 60%)
5
Explainable AI
Pearson correlation reveals relationships between features and historical dropout labels.
6
Intervention
Attendance recovery, remedial classes, peer tutoring, engagement programs, financial aid, backlog clearance.

🎯 Risk Classification Model

Students are dynamically classified into three risk tiers based on their predicted dropout probability \(P(\text{Dropout})\):

🟢 LOW RISK

< 30% Probability

Student shows strong academic performance and active engagement. Standard routine monitoring recommended.

🟠 MEDIUM RISK

30% – 59% Probability

Student shows early warning signs (e.g. declining attendance or assignment drops). Advisory counselor check-in recommended.

🔴 HIGH RISK

≥ 60% Probability

Critical dropout risk detected. Immediate targeted intervention required (parent alerts, remedial camps, peer tutoring).
